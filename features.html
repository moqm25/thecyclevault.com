Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

The CycleVault is engineered to be the calmest period tracker you've ever used. Every feature lives on your phone, runs without an account, and respects your attention.

Private tracking

Log flow, symptoms, discharge, mood and notes with calm, oversized controls.

  • One-tap "Start period" with haptic confirmation
  • 20+ symptom chips, discharge color & consistency
  • Optional free-text notes per day

On-device predictions

Predict your next period and current phase entirely on your phone.

  • Smoothed prediction window, not a single date
  • Honest "likely" language — never guarantees
  • Adapts as your history grows

Calm calendar

Past periods, predictions, and phases on the same gentle grid.

  • Three states: logged, today, predicted
  • Phase dots: menstrual, follicular, ovulatory, luteal
  • Swipe months with decelerating motion

Total data control

Your history is yours. Take it with you. Or wipe it instantly.

  • Export full history to portable JSON
  • Import from a JSON backup
  • One-tap "Delete all data" (irreversible)

Face ID lock

An extra layer between your cycle and anyone holding your phone.

  • Face ID / Touch ID unlock
  • Auto-lock when the app backgrounds
  • No CycleVault-side passcode storage

No account, ever

Open the app, start tracking. That's the entire onboarding.

  • No email, password, or phone number
  • No Sign-in-with-Apple linking your cycle to your ID
  • Optional first name — stored locally, used only inside the app

Zero third-party SDKs

No analytics, no ad networks, no crash reporters. You can audit the binary.

  • No Facebook SDK, no Google Analytics
  • No outbound network calls during normal use
  • Privacy summary documented in plain English

Deep insights SUPPORTER

Nine sections of pattern-finding from your last six cycles — predictions, averages, and patterns you can actually read.

  • Predictions: next 3 periods, fertile window, current phase
  • Personal averages: cycle length, period length, regularity score
  • Trend charts: cycle length over time + phase distribution
  • Symptom patterns: frequency and timing in your cycle
  • Year-at-a-glance + auto-generated observations

Doctor-friendly PDF SUPPORTER

One-tap export of your last 6 cycles into a clear, printable PDF designed to share with a healthcare provider.

  • Cycle history with dates and durations
  • Symptom log + flow patterns
  • Phase breakdown summary

Themes & app icons SUPPORTER

Make the app feel like yours. Four palettes that tint the entire app, three alternate home screen icons.

  • 4 color palettes: Coral, Sage, Ocean, Plum
  • 3 home screen icons: Default, Stealth, Minimal
  • Light / Dark / System mode (free for everyone)

Home screen widgets SUPPORTER

Glance at your cycle without opening the app — discreet widgets that respect your privacy.

  • Current cycle day & phase
  • Days until next period
  • Available in small and medium sizes
