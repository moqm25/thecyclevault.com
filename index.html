Cycle data is some of the most sensitive information you have. It shouldn't live on someone else's servers, get sold to brokers, or quietly train a model.

Designed for calm

A tracker that breathes with you.

Every screen is built to be quiet, fast, and unambiguous. No streaks. No nudges. No noise.

01

A home screen that calms, not counts.

Today is the first thing you see — your phase, your prediction, a single tap to log. No streaks, no notifications, no guilt loop. Just the rhythm of your body, rendered gently.

  • Live phase ring with soft predictive halo
  • One-tap "Start period" with haptic confirmation
  • Quick log: flow, mood, symptoms — under 5 seconds
02

A calendar you can read in a glance.

Past periods, today, and predicted windows live on the same calm grid. Coral dots are real. Dashed circles are predictions. The little ones underneath whisper your phases.

  • Three states: logged · today · predicted
  • Phase dots: menstrual, follicular, ovulatory, luteal
  • Swipe months with smooth, decelerating motion
03

Your history, beautifully detailed.

Tap any cycle to open a clean ledger of daily logs — flow, mood, symptoms, discharge — each saved as soft pills. Edit anything; nothing is permanent unless you say so.

  • Per-cycle summary: length, regularity, flow profile
  • Daily cards with editable symptom and color pills
  • Cycle-over-cycle comparison at a tap
04

Insights without the dashboard fatigue.

A single, calm chart of your last six cycles — and the one number that matters today: when your next period is likely to begin. We don't predict your fertility. We trust your body.

  • Cycle length trend with smooth spline interpolation
  • Current phase, with gentle plain-language context
  • "Likely" — never "guaranteed". Predictions stay honest.
05

Locked when you want it locked.

Turn on biometric lock and your cycle becomes invisible to anyone holding your phone — partners, family, customs officers. Face ID and Touch ID, no passcode storage of our own.

  • Face ID / Touch ID lock with instant unlock
  • Optional auto-lock on app background
  • Decoy mode (planned): replace the icon, hide the app

Questions, answered

Frequently asked.

Do you sell or share my data?

No. We never sell your data — and more importantly, we don't have access to it. Your cycle history lives on your phone in encrypted storage. There's no server-side database to sell from.

Is my data stored in the cloud?

By default, no. Everything is stored locally. If you have iCloud Backup turned on for your device generally, your encrypted CycleVault data may be included in that backup — controlled by Apple's encryption keys, not ours.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no accounts to create, ever. You open the app and you start tracking. No email, password, phone number, or Sign-in-with-Apple required.

How does The CycleVault make money?

The core experience — tracking, calendar, history, export, app lock, light/dark themes — is free forever. An optional Supporter tier ($4.99/mo, $39.99/yr, or $79.99 one-time) unlocks deeper analytics, doctor-friendly PDF export, custom themes, home screen widgets, and priority email support. No ads. No data deals.

What's included in Supporter?

The full Insights tab (predictions for next 3 periods, fertile window, current phase, personal averages, cycle length trend, phase distribution, symptom frequency + timing, auto-generated observations, year-at-a-glance grid), doctor-friendly PDF export of your last 6 cycles, 4 color themes that tint the entire app, 3 alternate home screen app icons, home screen widgets, and priority email support. New subscribers get a 14-day free trial on annual or 7-day on monthly.

Will it work on Android?

iOS first. Android is on the roadmap once the core experience is polished and stable on iOS.
